# Style canon

## Rendering core

- Semi-realistic anime proportions and recognizable silhouettes. Use thin dark brown-black ink contours with restrained variation in line weight.
- Render light and shadow as crisp cel shapes. Use saturated color blocks and a complementary relationship suited to the scene. Keep the brief's lighting intent and avoid gratuitous bloom, flare or airbrushed gradients.
- Use a tactile, dry gouache-like finish. Apply scuffs, wash blotches, chipped paint or rust only where materials and the brief call for them. Keep clean surfaces tactile through subtle texture.
- On a suitable visible human face, use warm matte skin, moderate eyes and rosy blush across the cheeks and nose bridge without changing identity or skin tone. Omit face treatment for obscured faces, faceless subjects and scenes without people.

## Optional scene defaults

When the brief leaves these open, try even daylight, an off-camera gaze, modest wide-angle perspective, a cropped foreground object, and lived-in layering. Choose scene-appropriate props. Night, distant figures, clean settings and spare compositions still use the same rendering core.

## Reference authority

The brief governs content. Authorized V3 anchors guide rendering, then this text canon, optional defaults, and provider heuristics. Use anchors for style traits without transplanting faces, objects or poses from them.
