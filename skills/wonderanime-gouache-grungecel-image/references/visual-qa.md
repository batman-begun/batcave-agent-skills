# Visual QA

1. Verify the brief and any locked source identity, composition and exact text.
2. Inspect applicable rendering traits: visible fine contours, hard-edged cel shadows, complementary color, dry surface treatment and recognizable facial identity. Mark scene-dependent traits inapplicable when appropriate.
3. Look for unintended glow, oily skin, oversized glossy eyes, cinematic grading, dirty surfaces in clean settings, garbled text and fused hands or objects.
4. Correct the largest visible defect first. Preserve the successful details and inspect the revised output before reporting fidelity.

For future calibration, record prompt, available anchors, host and model, output, and observed corrections. Use a portrait, a scene without a close human face, and a reference-based edit.
