# QUALITY GATES — prevent plausible premature closure

The gates below are *self-audit prompts*, not enforceable runtime rules. Passing one does not imply full internet coverage or independent scientific validation. Use proportional standards: brief facts should not trigger large research programs; high-stakes strategy deserves stronger scrutiny.

## G0 — framing integrity

- Distinguish user objective/constraints from assumed scope and seed examples.
- Were at least two meaningfully different framings considered in open-ended research?
- What outcome would make exploration useful to the user?

## G1 — discovery breadth

- Was the landscape approached from top-down **and** source-led bottom-up when relevant?
- Is there an outside-the-seeds lane and at least one cross-domain or contrarian perspective?
- Do the lanes differ in causal assumption, source population or stakeholder—not simply query phrasing?
- If not, list missing dimensions; do not silently present a complete landscape.

## G2 — evidence validity

- Is each recommendation-critical factual claim anchored to a source actually inspected?
- Is material evidence recent enough, in the correct geography, and truly relevant to the claim?
- Are inference, hypothesis and unknown separated? Are multiple articles tracing to one primary source treated as one lineage?

## G3 — challenge efficacy

- Was a fresh counter-search or alternate framing attempted, where tools and scope permit?
- Were alternative explanations, substitutes and failure cases sought?
- Are material contradictions resolved or made visible?

## G4 — blind-spot recovery

- What consequential perspectives, source classes or search geographies remain missing?
- Did new verified surprises create a new lane? Was that lane investigated or parked with reason?
- Did the latest searches add qualified candidates, or were they merely repeat hits from the same index?

## G5 — recommendation honesty

- Are claims of uniqueness, market absence, revenue, adoption, API freebies or feasibility appropriately qualified?
- Does each important hypothesis have an inexpensive next falsification test?
- Does reporting clearly separate **verified result**, **promising conjecture**, and **uninvestigated space**?

## Stop conditions

**QUALIFIED:** Relevant gates substantively checked; remaining gaps don't change practical conclusion given mission and budget. **PARTIAL:** budget/tool/time limit reached, inaccessible key sources, or unresolved material gaps; report usable discoveries and known limitations without claiming completeness. **EARLY HYPOTHESIS:** exploratory ideas only, little/no external testing. Never infer a numerical percentage of global coverage from search saturation. Silence about missing source classes is not a pass.

## Lean test for this skill

In API hunts: Can a new provider *class* still be easily surfaced by obvious independent search? In startup discovery: Can an independent outsider generate high-value orthogonal categories absent from this map? In both: What is the false positive rate of specific claims and are claim classes correctly labeled? New candidates alone aren't success if hallucinations grow.
